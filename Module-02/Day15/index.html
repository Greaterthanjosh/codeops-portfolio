OUR STORY

A Taste of Home in Bole

Habesha Eatery was created to celebrate the rich culinary traditions of Ethiopia. From our freshly prepared injera to our carefully seasoned wot and tibs, every dish is inspired by the flavors shared around Ethiopian family tables.

Whether you are enjoying a meal with family, meeting friends, or experiencing Ethiopian cuisine for the first time, our goal is to make every visit feel like coming home.
